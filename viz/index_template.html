Read this honestly: point color shows goals scored, not temperature — and the biggest pattern here is era, not climate. Scoring fell from 5.4 goals/match in 1954 to 2.2 in 1990 as tactics tightened, a swing far larger than any weather effect. Geography alone cannot isolate weather: host selection, altitude, kickoff time and match stage all confound it. The next step joins real per-match temperature and humidity from Open-Meteo, then controls for era and venue before claiming any weather signal.
